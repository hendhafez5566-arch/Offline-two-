Elhafez Tourism v32.5.12 — Entity-Backed State Storage

- Operational/accounting entity collections are persisted in erp_entity_records.
- erp_state switches to compact entity-backed storage only after row-count verification inside the transaction.
- Unsafe legacy payloads remain legacy-full automatically.
- Reads fail closed on entity-backed inconsistency instead of returning partial data.
- Backup/restore, critical state history, archive close, setup/reset, vendor security and email recovery use hydrated state and centralized persistence.
